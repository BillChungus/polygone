Linden & Co.

A made-up shop for trying Polygone

Linden & Co. does not exist. These pages copy the way a clothing shop lists a product, so you can see what the Polygone extension does. Install it, open a page below, and look at the corner of the page.

  • Odette Wrap Dress: one red box: all of the fabric is plastic-based
  • Marlowe Linen Shirt: one green box: no plastic fibres
  • Harbour Rib Tee: one orange box: a little plastic (5%)
  • Ridley Field Jacket: three boxes, one for each part: green shell, red lining, orange pockets
  • Wren Boxy Top: one grey box: no fabric information on the page

Click a box to see exactly what Polygone read. The toolbar icon turns it off for a site.
